🔄 HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For Every Email:
1. DETECTION → Scan for medical or bill-related keywords
2. EXTRACTION → Pull out amounts, dates, providers
3. CATEGORIZATION → Assign category (action_needed/reference)
4. PRIORITIZATION → Boost priority based on urgency
5. ACTION ITEMS → Generate concrete tasks
6. TAGGING → Add "medical", "health", "finance", "urgent", etc
7. LEARNING → Local intelligence learns from LLM classifications

Works even WITHOUT LLM:
├─ Fallback heuristics detect medical & bill keywords
├─ Basic extraction of amounts and dates
├─ Smart prioritization
└─ Concrete action item generation

Works BETTER WITH LLM:
├─ Semantic understanding of complex emails
├─ Better extraction of nuanced information
├─ More accurate categorization
└─ Learns patterns over time via local intelligence
